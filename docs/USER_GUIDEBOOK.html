Start here

Four basics to keep in mind before your first scan.

01 / ReviewOCR is a draft

Check every item yourself; camera text can be wrong or unavailable.

02 / ConfirmYou control analysis

The app analyzes only a non-empty ingredient list that you explicitly confirm.

03 / InterpretNo score or guarantee

Results cover only the rules and evidence currently configured.

The six-step flow

  1. Create and verify an account, or sign in.
  2. Optionally add personal notes to your profile.
  3. Upload a clear photo of the product's ingredient label.
  4. Correct the OCR draft or type the list manually, one ingredient per line.
  5. Confirm the list, then choose Analyze confirmed list.
  6. Read the limitations and citations; decide what follow-up is appropriate for you.
01

Create an account and set up your space

Your account keeps your scans in a private, user-specific workspace.

Create, verify, and sign in

  1. Choose Create account and enter an email address you can access.
  2. Create a unique password of at least 12 characters. Do not reuse a password from another service.
  3. Follow the verification instructions sent to your email. If the message is missing, check spam/junk or use the verification page to request new instructions.
  4. Sign in after verification. Use Forgot password? if you need to reset your password; one-time reset tokens should be kept private and used only once.
Keep access private. Do not share your password or verification/reset token with anyone. The app interface does not collect provider API keys; do not enter those in a user message or support request.

Optional profile notes

The optional profile can hold a display name, a self-reported skin-type selection, locale, and up to ten personal notes. You may skip onboarding and edit the profile later.

These entries are user-reported context—not medical records, verified allergies, or a diagnosis. The interface says profile notes are not sent to explanation providers. Add only information you are comfortable storing in your account.

02

Scan a product label

Choose a clear image so you can compare the text with the package.

1

Prepare the image

Use a well-lit, in-focus photo with the ingredient panel filling most of the frame. Keep the full text visible and avoid glare, steep angles, or motion blur.

  • Accepted formats: JPEG, PNG, or WebP.
  • Maximum file size: 10 MB.
  • Photograph only what is needed; avoid including faces, receipts, addresses, or unrelated personal information.
2

Upload the label

From your private workspace, select New scan or Scan a label. Choose Browse files or drag the image into the upload area, then select Upload label photo.

The service validates and re-encodes the image to remove embedded metadata. The image is associated with your account and is not sent to OpenAI or Groq as part of an explanation request.

3

Wait for the review draft

A scan may show Queued or Reading label before the review form appears. If it remains processing, return to Scan history and reopen it. OCR may be unavailable in a particular deployment; manual entry is still possible.

✓
Current build limitation

OCR is not enabled in the current application image. Until a reviewed OCR build is available, enter the ingredient text manually. Even when OCR is enabled in a later deployment, always verify it against the package.

03

Review and confirm the ingredients

This is the most important step: the app does not decide where one ingredient ends and another begins.

Correct the draft

  • Open View raw OCR text if you want to compare it with the image. Raw OCR is unconfirmed text.
  • In the ingredient box, enter one ingredient per line. Correct spelling, add missing items, remove text that is not an ingredient, and keep label order if possible.
  • Empty lines are ignored. The interface accepts up to 300 items; keep each ingredient line to 300 characters or fewer.
Water Glycerin Niacinamide Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride

Do not guess an ingredient name from an unclear photo. If you cannot read it, leave it unresolved or retake the image.

04

Understand your results

Results are narrow, rule-based notes—not a product rating or personal assessment.

How matching works

The app uses exact normalized ingredient records and a configured deterministic rule set. It does not make a fuzzy guess. Each displayed citation is checked by the server against current reviewed evidence/source records.

If a name is unresolved or ambiguous, the app does not invent a canonical identity or guessed finding. Review the spelling and label; if you still cannot identify it, treat it as unknown rather than assuming it is absent.

05

Optional explanations

An explanation may help unpack one existing finding, but it is not the source of truth.

Request an explanation

  1. Open a current finding in a confirmed scan.
  2. Select an available provider from the menu and choose Explain this finding.
  3. For OpenAI or Groq, read the displayed disclosure and give separate, provider-specific consent if you choose. Consent alone does not send anything; you must still request the explanation.
  4. You can revoke cloud-provider consent under Settings & privacy.

What an explanation can—and cannot—do

✓It can summarize one current deterministic finding using approved evidence text.
✓Only the selected finding's confirmed ingredient, current finding text, and approved evidence summaries/permitted excerpts may be sent to the chosen explanation provider.
×It cannot add, remove, or change a rule finding, and it cannot supply or replace a citation.
×Raw images, your email, full scan, other scan ingredients, source URLs, and profile/skin notes are not part of the explanation request.
×Generated text is not stored by the app and may be incomplete or inaccurate. It is not medical advice.
Ollama is an operator-configured endpoint shown as local in the interface. OpenAI and Groq are cloud providers and require current provider-specific consent. Do not enter provider passwords or API keys in the app; credentials are configured by the operator.
06

History, privacy, and account controls

Review and manage your data from the signed-in workspace.

Find or remove a scan

  • Overview shows recent scans. Scan history lists scans saved to your account; open one to return to its draft or results.
  • Use Delete beside a scan and confirm the action to remove that scan, its analysis history, and its retained private photo. Deletion cannot be undone.
  • The current interface loads the newest scan history; older-page navigation may not be available in this build.

Export or delete your account

  • Open Settings & privacy and select Download account export for a JSON copy of account/profile data, declarations, scans, and analysis history.
  • Delete account permanently removes the account, scans, analysis history, declared terms, and retained private photos. The app asks for your password and a final confirmation.
  • Sign out when you finish, especially on a shared device. Do not save passwords on a device you do not control.
i
Retention must be confirmed before public use.

The owner-approved backup policy is encrypted daily backups with 30-day retention, but the backup destination, schedule, alerting, and restore process are not configured or verified. This guide is not a promise about how quickly deleted data disappears from backups. The service operator must publish an accurate privacy/retention notice before launch.

Optional declared terms

In My profile, you may save ingredient terms marked as sensitivity, allergy, preference, or avoid. These are personal, user-reported notes. They are not medically verified and do not confirm an allergy or guarantee that a product will be filtered safely.

07

Troubleshooting

Common issues and safe next steps.

My photo is rejected

Check that it is a JPEG, PNG, or WebP image and is no larger than 10 MB. If it is still rejected, retake or export a smaller image. Avoid renaming a different file type to make it appear to be an accepted format.

The OCR text is blank, wrong, or unavailable

Compare the raw text with the package. Correct, add, or remove entries yourself, one per line. If recognition is unavailable, type the list manually. Never confirm a draft you have not reviewed.

An ingredient appears unresolved or ambiguous

Check the spelling and the exact text on the label. The app uses exact matching and does not guess. If the label is unclear, keep the identity unresolved rather than selecting a similar-sounding name.

I see “No active rules” or “No configured findings”

This is not a product safety result. It means the current database/rule coverage did not provide a finding or could not assess the list. Do not use it to decide that a product is safe for you.

A scan is still processing

Return to Scan history and reopen it. If it continues to fail, try a new clear label image. Processing can be interrupted if the local service restarts; no long-term processing guarantee is made by this draft guide.

I did not receive a verification or reset message

Check your spam/junk folder, verify that you entered the correct address, and request new instructions from the relevant page. Keep one-time tokens private. If the issue continues, use the service operator's published support route once one is available.

An optional explanation failed or seems incorrect

Read the deterministic finding and its citation instead. Generated prose may be unavailable, incomplete, or inaccurate and never overrides the finding. Do not retry by sending private data to another provider.

08

Keyboard and mobile use

The interface is designed to be navigable without a mouse.

  • Use Tab and Shift + Tab to move between links and controls; press Enter or Space to activate a control.
  • On a narrow screen, use the menu button to open the workspace navigation. Press Escape to close an open menu or dialog.
  • In a dialog, keyboard focus stays within the dialog until it closes.
  • Use the visible focus outline to track your position. You can print this guide or save it as a PDF from your browser.

Keyboard support does not by itself establish accessibility conformance. The product still requires broader device, screen-reader, zoom, and user testing.

09

Glossary

A few terms you may see in the app.

OCR
Optical character recognition: software that attempts to read text from an image. Its output must be checked by you.
Confirmed list
The ingredient list you have reviewed and explicitly confirmed. Only this list is eligible for deterministic analysis.
Deterministic rule
A preconfigured rule that produces the same result for the same confirmed input and rule version. It is not a medical judgment.
Citation
A source reference linked to a configured finding. It provides context, not a guarantee that a result applies to your individual situation.
Unresolved
The text did not match an exact ingredient record available to the app.
Ambiguous
The text could refer to more than one record, so the app does not guess which one you meant.
Stale analysis
An earlier result is no longer current because a source, rule, or catalog record changed. Refresh it before reading current findings.
